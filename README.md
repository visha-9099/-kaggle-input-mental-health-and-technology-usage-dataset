🧠📱 Mental Health and Technology Usage Dataset
This repository contains a detailed dataset on mental health and technology usage, exploring the impact of 
digital devices, social media, and screen time on mental well-being. The dataset provides valuable insights for
mental health professionals, data scientists, researchers, and policymakers interested in the relationship between
technology and mental health.

📌 Features
📊 Dataset Overview
Demographics: Age, gender, occupation, location.
Technology Usage: Daily screen time, smartphone/laptop/tablet usage, social media engagement.
Mental Health Indicators: Anxiety, depression, stress levels, sleep quality.
Lifestyle Factors: Physical activity, diet, work-life balance.
Productivity & Focus: Impact of technology on work efficiency.
Self-Reported Mental Health Conditions: Diagnosed conditions and self-assessment scores.
